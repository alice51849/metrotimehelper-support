Taipei Metro guide for visitors: lines, last trains and when to leave

The Taipei Metro, also called the Taipei MRT, is how most visitors get around Taipei. The hard part is timing: how early to leave for a dinner reservation, a tour pickup or the last train back to your hotel when a transfer is involved. These answers come from the App Store description of Metro Time Helper, an unofficial Taipei Metro app.

Work out when to leave in three steps

  1. Choose your starting station and destination, or tap a station on the built-in zoomable MRT map.
  2. Enter the time you need to arrive.
  3. See the latest time to walk into the station and a step-by-step timeline for every leg and transfer. Would rather leave right now? Switch to Leave now and see when you will get there.

Last trains on the Taipei Metro

Last trains differ by station and direction, so this page does not give one time. Plan the trip in the app instead: the timeline shows first and last train warnings, and once you set Remind me you get a reminder when it is time to go. A Live Activity counts down on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (iPhone only). For exact service hours, check the operator's official information before you travel. More in Taipei MRT last train Q&A.

Two branch lines run on their own fixed timetable: Beitou to Xinbeitou (the hot spring area) and Qizhang to Xiaobitan. The app has a separate mode for each, with a countdown to the next train, full timetables for today, weekdays and weekends, departure reminders 1 to 30 minutes ahead, and the official timetable PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Taipei Metro the same as the Taipei MRT?

Yes. Many visitors say Taipei Metro, and signs and apps often say Taipei MRT. Both mean the same rapid transit network in Taipei. Metro Time Helper is built for it and works backward from the time you need to arrive, transfers included, to tell you the latest time to walk into the station.

Which Taipei Metro lines does the app cover?

Wenhu, Tamsui-Xinyi (including the Xinbeitou branch), Songshan-Xindian (including the Xiaobitan branch), Zhonghe-Xinlu, Bannan, phase one of the Circular line, and the New Taipei Metro Sanying line (Dingpu to Yingge). The Airport MRT and light rail are not included, so plan your airport trip separately.

Can it tell me when the last Taipei Metro train is?

Last trains differ by station and direction, so this page does not list one time. In the app you choose your start, your destination and the time you need to arrive; the trip timeline shows first and last train warnings, and with Remind me set you get a reminder when it is time to go. For exact service hours, check the operator's official information before you travel.

Are the times live arrivals?

No. Times are estimates based on official published headways and travel times between stations, not live train arrivals. Pick Standard estimates (half a headway of waiting) or Cautious estimates (a full headway), and adjust your station entry buffer to leave yourself more margin.

Does it work in English or my own language?

Yes. The app comes in 50 languages you can switch inside the app. The official Taipei MRT network map is in the app as a zoomable vector map in Chinese or English (the map needs a connection).

Is there a subscription?

On the App Store it is a paid download: buy once and every feature is available from the very first launch, with no subscription and no in-app purchases. A paid version is also on Google Play; check its listing for Android details.

Is this an official Taipei Metro app?
