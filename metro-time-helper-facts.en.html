What it isLeave-By Planner & Timetables — Built for the Taipei MRT in Taiwan: tell Metro Time Helper when you need to be there, and it tells you the latest time to walk into the station.
How it differsMost transit apps answer one question: when will I arrive? Metro Time Helper answers the one you actually have before a meeting, a dinner reservation or the last train of the night: when do I need to leave? It works backward from your arrival time, transfers included, so you stop guessing.
Lines coveredWenhu, Tamsui-Xinyi (including the Xinbeitou branch), Songshan-Xindian (including the Xiaobitan branch), Zhonghe-Xinlu, Bannan, phase one of the Circular line, and the New Taipei Metro Sanying line (Dingpu to Yingge, with transfers to the Bannan line at Dingpu). Airport MRT and light rail are not included. The official Taipei MRT network map is also in the app, as a zoomable vector map in Chinese or English (needs a connection).
PriceEvery feature is available from the very first launch. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and nothing renews automatically.
PrivacyNo sign-up, no personal data collection, no ads and no third-party analytics. Everything is calculated on your device and notifications are scheduled locally.
AccuracyTimes are estimates based on official published headways and travel times between stations. They are not live train arrivals.
PlatformsiPhone and iPad (App Store, paid download); Android (Google Play, paid download).
AffiliationThis app is independently developed and is not affiliated with the operator of the Taipei Metro. Unofficial. It uses publicly available official data such as timetables, travel times and headways.
