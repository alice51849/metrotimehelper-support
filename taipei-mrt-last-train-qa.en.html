Taipei MRT last train: when do you need to leave?

Out at a night market and wondering if you can still make the last train back to your hotel? Need to be somewhere by 7 pm and not sure when to head to the station? These answers come from the App Store description of Metro Time Helper, an unofficial Taipei MRT app.

Who it is for

  • First-time visitors to Taipei who are new to MRT transfers
  • Travelers with a dinner reservation, tour pickup or show to make on time
  • Anyone heading back late who wants first and last train warnings
  • Day trippers taking the branch line to the Xinbeitou hot springs

Find the latest time to walk into the station in three steps

  1. Choose your starting station and destination (or tap a station on the built-in zoomable MRT map).
  2. Enter the time you need to arrive.
  3. See the latest time to enter the station and a step-by-step timeline for every leg and transfer. Pick Standard estimates (half a headway of waiting) or Cautious estimates (a full headway), and adjust your station entry buffer.

Frequently asked questions

Can it tell me when the last Taipei MRT train is?

Last trains differ by station and direction, so this page does not list one time. In the app you choose your start, your destination and the time you need to arrive; the timeline shows first and last train warnings, and with Remind me set you get a reminder when it is time to go. Times are estimates based on official published headways and travel times between stations, not live train arrivals, so leave yourself a margin late at night.

Does it have a Taipei MRT schedule or timetable?

For two branch lines, yes: Qizhang to Xiaobitan and Beitou to Xinbeitou (the hot spring area) use the official fixed timetable, with a countdown to the next train, full timetables for today, weekdays and weekends, departure reminders 1 to 30 minutes ahead, and the official timetable PDF. For the rest of the network, trip times are estimated from official published headways and travel times between stations.

How is this different from a regular route app?

Most transit apps answer when you will arrive. Metro Time Helper works backward from the time you need to be there, transfers included, and tells you the latest time to walk into the station. If you would rather leave right now, switch to Leave now and see when you will get there.

Does it work in English?

Yes. The app comes in 50 languages you can switch inside the app. The official Taipei MRT network map is also in the app as a zoomable vector map in Chinese or English (the map needs a connection).

Which lines are covered? Does it include the Airport MRT?

Wenhu, Tamsui-Xinyi (including the Xinbeitou branch), Songshan-Xindian (including the Xiaobitan branch), Zhonghe-Xinlu, Bannan, phase one of the Circular line, and the New Taipei Metro Sanying line (Dingpu to Yingge). The Airport MRT and light rail are not included, so plan your airport trip separately.

Can I see it on my Lock Screen or Apple Watch?

Once you set Remind me, a Live Activity counts down to your time to leave or your next departure on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (iPhone only). There is also a Metro Time widget and a standalone Apple Watch app with all three modes, countdowns and watch face complications.

Do I need an account? What data does it collect?

No sign-up, no personal data collection, no ads and no third-party analytics. Everything is calculated on your device and notifications are scheduled locally.

Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?

Not in the App Store version. It is a paid download: buy once and every feature is available from the very first launch, with no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, and nothing that renews automatically. A paid version is also on Google Play; check its listing for Android details.

Is this the official Taipei Metro app?

No. This app is independently developed and is not affiliated with the operator of the Taipei Metro. Unofficial. It uses publicly available official data such as timetables, travel times and headways.
